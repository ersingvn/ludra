# Moonlit Shrine

## Promise

A silent lake at night: you carry the only warm light and bring the moon back piece by piece.

## Look

- style: low-poly night in violet and moon white, every warm light a small event
- type: a calm serif over the scene, moons instead of numbers
- palette: #bfefff, #2b3140, #ffe0a8, #a4adbd, #ffa640, #e2502e, #c9b8ff, #4a3a78
