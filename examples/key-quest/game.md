# Key Quest

## Promise

A quiet dungeon where every locked door is a promise and every key a small win.

## Look

- style: warm top-down torchlight on stone, with rich colors in the dark
- type: a storybook serif display face, keys shown as icons
- palette: #3b2c52, #7a4e2d, #5fbf6a, #f2c14e, #cfd8ff, #fff3b0, #56658f, #ff6b5d
