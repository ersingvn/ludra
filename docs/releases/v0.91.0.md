# v0.91.0: 3D

Ludra makes 3D games now, through the same loop as 2D: JSON scenes, `check`, `play`, `test`, `review` and real screenshots. As in 2D, the AI gets the real tools instead of a ceiling: Three.js for looks and Rapier 3D for physics.

```bash
npx @ludra/cli new my-game --template 3d
```

![Sky Islands](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ersingvn/ludra/main/docs/media/sky-islands.png)

## 3D worlds

`"world": { "dimensions": 3 }` makes a scene 3D: metres, +y up, +z toward the camera, gravity 9.81 m/s².
- Transforms get `z` and a rotation per axis.
- Bodies get `depth` and 3D shapes: sphere, capsule, cylinder, convex hulls.
- Tests, `play --watch` and scripts carry the third dimension.
- Validation keeps 2D and 3D apart, with a fix for each mistake.

## Rapier 3D

Every 3D scene runs on Rapier 3D's deterministic build, loaded only by 3D scenes.
- **Joints:** hinges around any axis (with limits and motors), sliders, welds, ropes, springs and ball joints.
- **Rules:** sensors, collectibles, hazards, stomps, checkpoints, goals and falling out of the world all work in 3D.
- **`character`, the 3D player:**
  - walks on the ground plane and faces where it walks;
  - jumps with coyote time, buffering and variable height;
  - climbs slopes and steps, pushes crates and rides moving platforms.
- **Determinism:** a golden hash pins the 3D simulation on Linux, Windows and macOS × Node 22 and 24.
  - macOS ARM computed `Math.atan2` a last digit differently, so 3D rotations now use core's new `dmath`: trigonometry from IEEE-exact operations that gives the same bits everywhere.

## `@ludra/three`, the renderer

A new package draws 3D scenes with Three.js, loaded only by 3D scenes.
- **`mesh`:** primitives or the body's own shape, physically based materials, tiling textures and shadows.
- **`light`:** a sun with soft shadows that follow the camera, sky fill, point, spot and ambient lights.
- **`camera3d`:** follow, orbit, first-person and fixed.
- **Scene `environment`:** a sky gradient, fog and tone mapping.

The 3D frame is drawn under the game's 2D layer, so `drawUI` HUDs, postfx shaders and screenshots work unchanged. `--debug` shows collider wireframes.

## No ceiling: the render hook

A script's `render(ctx)` hook gets the real three.js every frame: the module, the scene, the camera, the renderer, and a group that moves with the entity.
- Custom geometry and shaders, instancing and particles.
- `ctx.addon()` loads any three.js addon, and `ctx.setRender()` takes over the frame, so a bloom or depth-of-field chain is a few lines.
- Like draw hooks it can't change the game, and `ludra check` runs it against stand-ins.

Game logic in 3D gets:
- vector impulses and forces (struck off-center, a body spins), `spin`, `rotationX`/`rotationY` and 3D raycasts;
- tweens along z and around x and y;
- the raw Rapier 3D API.

## Sky Islands

`--template 3d` is a 3D platformer:
- Hop between floating islands above a sea of clouds and collect five crystals.
- Light the beacon, ride the raft across the widest gap, dodge (or stomp) the storm puff, and step through the portal.

Its hero, clouds, beacon and portal are render hooks. It has its own sounds and music, ten tests, and a clean `review --strict`.

`ludra review` knows 3D: it asks for the game's own lights, sky and materials, and points out solid bodies nobody can see.

## Fixed on the way

- Patrols could stop at their far end when the end position was a rounding error short.
- Characters could get stuck on kinematic platforms after sinking a hair into the controller's gap on landing.

## Upgrading

- `npm install @ludra/cli@^0.91.0`. 2D games simulate exactly as before: every test of every example and template hashes frame for frame the same as v0.9.0.
- `@ludra/three` is new; the CLI depends on it and loads it only for 3D scenes.
